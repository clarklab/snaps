# Bundled font

**Google Sans Flex** — © Google LLC, licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License v1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

Four weights (Regular / Medium / SemiBold / Bold) are self-hosted here and
loaded via `@font-face` in `src/index.css`, so the app renders correctly
offline. The OFL permits bundling and redistribution within an application.
Source: <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex>.
